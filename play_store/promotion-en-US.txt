DEVELOPER FEEDBACK POST
Hi! I'm building Secar, an independent Android app for following F1, WEC, IMSA, IndyCar and GT racing.

It brings event calendars, session times, published results and standings into one app. You can choose your championships, switch seasons and display session times in your own time zone.

The app is in open testing on Google Play. No account is required, and there are no in-app ads. I'd especially appreciate feedback from people who follow several series: is it easy to find the next session, the results and the championship you care about?

Disclosure: I make the app; this is a developer feedback request, not an independent review. Results coverage depends on the organizers' publications.
