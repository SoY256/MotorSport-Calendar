featureGraphic: Secar motorsport calendars, results and standings with a preview of the actual app.
01-weekend: Race event list with series selection, a countdown to the next session and event times.
02-results: WEC race results at Monza showing positions, crews, teams, classes and times.
03-standings: F1 driver standings for the selected season, with points and gaps to the leader.
04-calendar: Monthly calendar with events color-coded by racing series.
05-seasons: Season switcher above the calendar of upcoming race events.
06-series: Settings for GT Europe, GT International, GT America, GT Asia, GT Australia and British GT.
