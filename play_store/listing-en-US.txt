APP NAME
Secar: Motorsport Calendar

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Race schedules, results and standings for F1, WEC, IMSA, IndyCar and GT

FULL DESCRIPTION
Your race weekend, all in one place. Secar brings together motorsport calendars, session schedules, results and championship standings so you can follow the series that matter to you.

FOLLOW YOUR SERIES
Keep up with Formula 1, Formula 2 and Formula 3, FIA WEC, IMSA WeatherTech, INDYCAR and INDY NXT. Add GT3 racing: GT World Challenge Europe with Endurance and Sprint Cup, America, Asia and Australia, Intercontinental GT Challenge and British GT.

PLAN THE WEEKEND
Browse events in a list or a monthly or weekly calendar. See session times in your own time zone or in the circuit's local time. A countdown shows how long remains until the next session, and you can hide completed events.

COME BACK FOR THE RESULTS
Browse published race, qualifying and practice classifications alongside driver and team championship standings. Event details also include circuit layouts and track information where available.

ONE SEASON AT A TIME
Switch between years instead of scrolling through one endless list. The calendar, results and standings stay within your selected season. Announced future calendars are added as their data becomes available.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose the championships you follow, pick a light or dark theme, and use the app in English or Polish. No account is required, and the app contains no advertising or user-activity tracking tools.

ABOUT THE DATA
Calendars and results are updated from public motorsport sources. Coverage and the timing of classifications depend on each series and the organizer's publications. Unconfirmed times are marked TBC. Updates require an internet connection; offline, the app uses the data included with your installed version.

Secar is an independent, unofficial application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the championships, organizers or teams shown. Names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Schedules and results may change; the relevant organizer publishes the authoritative information.
