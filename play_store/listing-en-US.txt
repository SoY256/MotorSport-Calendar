APP NAME (5/30)
Secar

SHORT DESCRIPTION (76/80)
Motorsport calendars, session times, results and standings in one place.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Follow the motorsport season without jumping between multiple websites.

Secar brings calendars, session schedules, event details, results and championship standings together in one clean application. Choose the series you follow and see only the information that matters to you.

FEATURES
• Formula 1, Formula 2 and Formula 3
• FIA World Endurance Championship (WEC)
• IMSA SportsCar Championship
• INDYCAR and INDY NXT
• List, monthly calendar and weekly calendar views
• Race, qualifying and practice results
• Driver and team championship standings
• Circuit layouts, track information and national flags
• Local time or circuit local time
• English and Polish language support
• Light and dark themes
• Manual refresh and regularly updated public motorsport data

Secar does not require an account and contains no advertising or user tracking.

Secar is an independent, unofficial motorsport reference application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the championships, organizers, teams or manufacturers shown in the application. Series names, team names, logos, driver images and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Schedules and results can change; consult the relevant organizer for official decisions.
